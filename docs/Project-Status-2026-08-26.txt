- After a volunteer declines an assignment, the declined task is removed from Active Assignments and remaining Open actions work.

LOCKED USER PATH BEHAVIOR
- During onboarding, users select one or more of: Public Speaking, Professional Presenting, Content Creation.
- Users may add or remove selected Paths later in Profile/Settings.
- Paths personalize the experience; they are not access restrictions.
- All 13 tracks remain browsable and available regardless of selected Paths.
- Path changes must not alter historical evaluations, Approved scores, reports, Track Mastery, or historical leaderboard/evaluation records.

LOCKED AI EVALUATION MVP PATH
- One Gemini evaluation attempt per AI evaluation request.
- Direct evaluation of the original submitted compliant video.
- Strict structured JSON output and basic structure/scoring-integrity validation.
- Usable valid result may auto-approve under publication rules.
- Failed, malformed, incomplete, invalid, or otherwise unusable AI result becomes Rejected.
- No automatic AI retry.
- AI-output failure does not enter Pending Moderation.
- Only Approved AI evaluations affect progress, leaderboards, and official .docx report generation.

LOCKED HUMAN EVALUATION / GOVERNANCE MODEL
- Super Admin → Admin → Volunteer Evaluator governance.
- Exactly one active Human Evaluator assignment per Human Evaluation request.
- Reassignment supersedes the previous assignment and transfers sole active ownership/access.
- Evaluator submission locks the structured version and moves work to Completed/History.
- Re-review is versioned and does not overwrite the prior version.
- Admins may moderate Human outcomes but cannot rewrite evaluator-authored criterion scores or feedback.
- Human Pending Moderation remains valid under the locked first-in-track/manual-review and later anomaly-trigger rules.

OTHER CORE MVP RULES STILL LOCKED
- 13-track scoring architecture under the 100-point rubric.
- Criterion-specific Low / Competent / Excellent anchors.
- Timestamped evidence, strength, weakness, actionable improvement advice, and Overall Summary in evaluations.
- Approved-only official .docx generation.
- AI and Human public leaderboards remain separate.
- Private mastery remains separate from public leaderboard ranking.
- Exact and near-duplicate/re-encoded submissions are allowed in MVP.

- Flutter + Riverpod + go_router for the mobile app.
